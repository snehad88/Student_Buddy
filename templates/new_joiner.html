Welcome to School! 👋

Here are some important school guidelines to help you get started.

🕐 School Timings

  • School starts at 8:20 AM.
  • Students should be present by 8:15 AM.
  • Primary, Middle & Secondary: 8:20 AM – 3:30 PM Monday to Friday.
  • Saturday: 8:20 AM – 12:30 PM.
  • The 2nd Saturday is a holiday.

🤝 Code of Conduct

  • Wear the prescribed school uniform and identity card.
  • Dress appropriately on special occasions and colour-dress days.
  • Treat teachers, elders and fellow students with respect.
  • Greet teachers and others with traditional "Namaste".
  • Keep classrooms, school buildings and the campus clean.
  • Use dustbins for waste disposal.
  • The school is a plastic-free zone.
  • Be punctual and reach school on time.
  • Do not bring mobile phones, valuables or prohibited publications to school.

📝 Leave Rules

  • Inform your class teacher about planned leave through a letter or diary note.
  • For emergency pickup, a request letter must be submitted at reception and permission obtained from the Principal.
  • If you are absent for more than three days because of illness, a medical certificate is required.
  • Keep your absence record updated in the school diary.
  • Attendance on the first and last working day around vacations is compulsory.
  • 75% attendance is mandatory.

📖 Library Rules

  • You can borrow one book per week.
  • Reference books and periodicals are not issued on loan.
  • Return books on time to avoid fines.
  • Do not write, underline, fold, tear or damage library books.
  • Newspapers and magazines should be read only in the library.
  • Maintain complete silence in the library.
🌟 Remember

Be punctual, respectful, responsible and take care of your school and its resources.
